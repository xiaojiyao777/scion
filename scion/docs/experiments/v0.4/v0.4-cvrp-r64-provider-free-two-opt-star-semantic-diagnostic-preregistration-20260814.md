1. `cut_delta_equivalence`: whether the unique def-used allowlisted delta AST

   `0`/end/interior cut pair.
2. `near_eps_decision_equivalence`: whether every separately frozen near-EPS
   case makes the same strict-improvement decision as the direct oracle.
3. `first_improvement_state_equivalence`: whether actual function execution
