current source, complete ordinary research history and problem-owned prior

## Stages and continuous-research interpretation

`--rounds 2` counts completed formal Protocol stages, not H/C attempts.

- If candidate 1 requests expansion, stage 2 reuses that exact verified branch
  on the six-case expanded screen. It does not invoke a new H or C.
- If candidate 1 returns to exploration, a later H may read its ordinary
  same-campaign observation and autonomously pivot or refine before a distinct
  candidate-2 initial screen.
- A short Contract/Verification/research rejection does not count as a formal
  stage, but every provider dispatch still consumes the shared cap.

The second path is adaptive continuous development, not independent
confirmation. Validation, frozen evaluation, promotion and retained replay are
zero in either path. Even an expanded-screen pass is development evidence only.

## Resources and stops

The unchanged research limits allow at most eight H turns and eight C research
actions plus one independent C final decision per attempt. Provider SDK retry
is zero. The shared cap remains 34; it is not increased because M24 failed.

The conservative envelope is:

- provider calls `<=34`; H turn timeout `120s`, C turn/final timeout `240s`;
- formal Protocol/Safe-Feature/Decision stages `<=2`;
- solver subprocesses `<=48`: Verification `8`, canary `4`, formal screen `36`;
- solver nominal/guarded subject-seconds `<=1900 / 2620`;
- public development pytest `<=450s`; Verification pytest `<=480s`;
- structured-provider timeout `<=7080s`;
- known guarded work `<=10630s`;
- campaign-run outer hardwall `14000s`; solver concurrency `1`.

These are structural upper bounds, not an asserted candidate count. The 34-call
cap still admits two maximally long H(8)+C(8+final) sessions, but rejected or
partial attempts may consume a different mix.

Provider-cap exhaustion is typed `resource_exhausted /
PROVIDER_CALL_CAP_EXHAUSTED` with no extra dispatch. H transcript/turn/result
cap exhaustion is also typed resource exhaustion and stops. Provider/transport
infrastructure, other `NOT_EVALUATED`, interrupt, unknown or
evaluated-without-formal outcomes stop the invocation. Outer hardwall returns
124 and terminates children. Shell exit zero alone is not scientific success;
terminal interpretation uses status, validity, evaluated rounds, last outcome
and stop reason.

There is no provider retry, patch repair, campaign resume, output-root reuse,
population replacement or automatic M26.

## Claim boundaries

Framework evidence may report which source/history refs H actually read,
whether fixed correction feedback enabled a valid basis, which bounded C
actions ran, and which unchanged gates were reached.

Research-effectiveness evidence is a post-hoc audit of nearest-prior use,
material difference, alternatives, falsifier, whether C implemented the stated
mechanism, and whether a second H used a same-campaign observation. Plausible
text alone is not evidence-grounded selection. Replaying an earlier mechanism
is a replay or refinement unless the cited material difference is supported.
This rubric neither selects a target nor gates Protocol.

Algorithm evidence requires a complete paired formal stage. Initial and
expanded screens are reported separately. Problem-owned family telemetry is at
most association, not exact activation or causal proof. No validation, frozen,
promotion, retained, global-CVRP, independent-discovery, production-readiness
or v0.4-complete claim follows.
