The last H call and C call both completed successfully. The C response had the
normal structured patch fields, including `file_path`, `old_string` and
`new_string`. Proposal application then failed because the requested
`exact_replace` target was absent from the current branch source:
`old_string_not_found`.

The recorded terminal projection was:

  `ProposalValidationError / response_parse_failed`;
- stored detail: `artifact:change_vehicle_type.py#a7cd3d3646f6`.

This was not a provider outage: all 32 provider calls were successful. It was
also not a Protocol decision, an evaluation failure or a scheduler exhaustion
condition. It was a candidate-local proposal/application miss before formal
evaluation. The framework incorrectly allowed that recoverable miss to stop
the campaign as `NOT_EVALUATED`; the two other v2 branches were still
schedulable. The appropriate research-loop behavior is to reject that H/C and
schedule a fresh H from the clean base, not to retry the failed C or terminate
the invocation.

The clean wrapper exit therefore does not mean the requested campaign
completed. It only means the process returned normally after the framework
misclassified the candidate-local miss as invocation-terminal.

## Claim boundary

R6 supports these claims:

- a fresh Warehouse agent generated one algorithm change that passed
  screening, validation and frozen evaluation and became champion v2;
- the protocol rejected fourteen later candidates instead of treating activity
  alone as improvement;
- research continued after promotion across three branches based on champion
  v2, with fourteen additional formal screenings;
- the H/C provider path remained stable for all 32 calls;
- the 17 evaluated stages are interpretable partial evidence with complete
  evidence and lineage records.

R6 does not support these stronger claims:

- that the 36 requested formal stages completed;
- that Scion achieved multiple promotions or continuous solver improvement;
- that any of the fourteen post-promotion candidates improved Warehouse
  performance;
- that stale-branch reconciliation was exercised successfully in this run;
- that the Warehouse result generalizes to VRP, production data or another
  problem population;
- that the terminal event was a provider transient;
- that the preregistered R6 scientific endpoint was achieved.

The concise R6 conclusion is: **one effective Warehouse improvement plus real
post-promotion research continuity, but not repeated optimization; the campaign
ended at 17/36 when `exact_replace: old_string_not_found` was incorrectly
promoted from a recoverable proposal miss to terminal `NOT_EVALUATED`.**
