## Fixed research context and operation surface

The provider receives research material, not a rule catalogue:

- H sees complete current editable B0/branch source, objective and
  feasibility semantics, current source-grounded solver mechanics, compact
  aggregate search-phase evidence, recent same-branch observations in detail,
  older observations structurally and sibling state briefly;
- H sees the neutral accepted CVRP prior, including known failed or unresolved
  directions, without a prescribed mechanism, action, target or demand for
  novelty;
- case feedback exposes anonymous observational CVRPLIB dimension/size bucket
  where derivable from the filename; path/case identity stays out of the H
  view, and these facts do not enter Decision;
- C sees the complete approved editable source and may use modify or create
  actions, ordered exact replacements, multiple declared file edits and the
  existing safe research tools;
- the prompt requests one evidence-grounded mechanism-level change or
  refinement. Same-branch refinement is valid; the host does not choose
  whether to refine or pivot.

Core hypothesis/patch/source, Verification, pair, Protocol and Decision lineage
is durable for replay and postrun analysis. Raw provider traces and call-journal
writes remain best-effort diagnostics and never discard an otherwise valid
provider result. The provider view is a stable semantic projection, not token-
triggered truncation, top-k selection or an opaque summary. No identity, lease,
issuance, hash closure, novelty, activation, telemetry-admission or algorithm-
quality gate is added.

The anonymous case feedback has no stable case alias, and current compact phase
telemetry is aggregate rather than case-by-phase. This is an explicit analysis
limit of the matched rung, not a completed context claim or a reason to add a
pre-Protocol gate. Postrun analysis will determine whether a later fresh design
needs a minimal observational case/phase association.

## Fixed Protocol and measurement semantics

- Verification owns import, execution, feasibility, objective semantics,
  determinism and declared behavioral invariants. Every verified candidate
  proceeds directly to Protocol.
- `fleet_violation` is the only protected objective. `total_distance` is the
  predeclared effect metric. Screening practical delta is `2.0`; validation
  practical delta is `1.0`.
- Screening requires case win rate at least `0.60` and median delta at least
  `2.0`. Validation requires case win rate at least `0.66`, median delta at
  least `1.0` and bootstrap CI low at least `0`. Frozen applies the same
  `0.66`/`1.0`/CI-low requirements and a passing canary. Exact implementation
  semantics remain those at the fixed source commit.
- Ordinary initial case-level uncertainty, or an initial practical sparse
  no-loss signal, may receive exactly the predeclared expanded population when
  the fixed gate deems expansion reachable. Expansion is not a pass and does
  not weaken the original pass threshold.
- Every expanded population contains the complete initial population with the
  same stage seeds. No current result chooses a case.
- Mechanism activation, elapsed shares, ALNS opportunities, accepted/best
  counts and runtime are proposal feedback and postrun evidence only. They do
  not admit, reject, rank or promote the current candidate.

The accepted A/A calibration reports screening `MDE@80%=9.9`, recommends at
least eight seeds, and reports a per-case p90 absolute noise diagnostic of
`45.5`. This campaign nevertheless retains four screening seeds to provide a
matched first test of the context/runtime corrections. Therefore:

- a screening pass supports only advancement to the next fixed Protocol stage;
- a fail can reject that exact candidate at this Protocol rung;
- a fail cannot rule out effects below the calibrated MDE, and especially
  cannot establish that no `2..9.9` distance improvement exists.

If completed evidence is directionally positive but near the gate, sparse or
otherwise power-limited, the only allowed power redesign is a separately
pre-registered fresh campaign. Whether to change screening, validation or
frozen populations and the exact additional seeds remain an analysis/design
question after this root; no seed is added inside this campaign.

## Stop and continuation policy

- Launch exactly once into the absent root. Never resume the root or retry an
  H/C call.
- Candidate-local schema/Contract rejection, invalid provider edit against the
  complete visible source, or Verification rejection consumes no formal stage
  and schedules a fresh H on clean current source. A post-Contract workspace
  materialization/filesystem failure, missing provider terminal response,
  local context/binding failure, infrastructure, resource or interruption
  remains terminal.
- A completed non-promoting screening retains the verified candidate as the
  branch's provisional current source under V3 section 11.2.
- Queued expanded screening, validation, expanded validation and frozen drain
  on the exact candidate with no new H/C.
- Continue until eight formal stages complete or a genuine terminal outcome
  occurs. If stage 8 leaves a queued exact candidate, seal the root and report
  horizon censoring; do not extend or resume it.
- Do not edit framework, ProblemSpec, Protocol, split, seed, threshold, context
  semantics or time rules while the run is active.

## Outcome classifications

Classifications are mutually exclusive in this precedence order: observed
promotion; queued exact-candidate horizon censoring; completed no-promotion
with no queued formal candidate; invalid infrastructure; explicit
interruption.

- `CVRP_PROMOTION_OBSERVED_REPLAY_PENDING`: at least one exact candidate passes
  screening, validation and frozen and receives `PROMOTE`; retained improvement
  still requires the independent B0 comparison.
- `VALID_8STAGE_FUNNEL_HORIZON_CENSORED`: the root completes eight stages with
  an exact candidate queued for a later formal stage. This is useful funnel
  evidence, not promotion.
- `VALID_8STAGE_NO_PROMOTION_LARGE_EFFECT_NOT_DEMONSTRATED`: all eight stages
  are interpretable, none promotes and no exact candidate remains queued for a
  later formal stage. This rejects the exact candidates at this fixed rung;
  sub-MDE improvement remains inconclusive.
- `RUN_INVALID_INFRA`: provider, configuration, framework, runner or resource
  failure truncates the declared horizon.
- `INTERRUPTED`: an explicit external interruption ends the run.

Postrun analysis must separately report agent research behavior, edit
causality/activation, search allocation, formal evidence, measurement power and
promotion state. No single gate result may substitute for that analysis.
