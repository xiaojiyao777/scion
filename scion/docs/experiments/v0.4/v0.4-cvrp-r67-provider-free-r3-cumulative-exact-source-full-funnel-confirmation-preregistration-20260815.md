## Frozen measurement and gates

For each same-case, same-seed pair the signed effect is
`B0 total_distance - candidate total_distance`; positive is better for the
candidate. Failed-subject objective values are inadmissible. Within a case,
the median of its paired effects determines case W/L/T with equivalence band
zero. Pair W/L/T remains descriptive. The protected objective is
`fleet_violation`; any candidate protected regression vetoes the gate.

Initial screening can never pass directly. It expands only if candidate failed
pairs are zero and either:

- `(case wins - case losses) / 8 >= 0.125`, case loss rate `<= 0.25`, and
  bootstrap CI high `>= 2`; or
- at least one case win, zero case losses, median improvement `>= 2`, and CI
  high `>= 2`.

The complete expanded screening rule requires all 12 cases, candidate failed
