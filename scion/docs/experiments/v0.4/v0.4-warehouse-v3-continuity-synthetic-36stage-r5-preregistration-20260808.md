```bash

  jq -er '.proxy_api_key | select(type == "string" and length > 0)')"
```

The extracted value is used for one no-output `/v1/models` HTTP-success check
and is then passed unchanged to the campaign child. There is no second `jq`
credential derivation and no fallback credential source. The value must never
be printed or persisted.

This is the only R5 launch difference from R4. Scientific inputs and executable
framework revision remain unchanged.

## Execution and stop policy

- Launch once into the declared fresh R5 campaign directory.
- Do not resume or retry R4, and do not resume a partial R5 root. Do not copy
  prior campaign state into R5.
- Do not retry a provider H or C call. A scheduler-forward research rejection
  starts a new H attempt from the selected clean base; it is not a retry of the
  rejected call.
- Do not stop at champion v2, at a negative candidate, or when a patchless old
  branch is retired. Continue until 36 typed stages complete or the invocation
  reaches a genuine terminal outcome.
- Do not modify framework source, solver source, problem/protocol/split/seed
  inputs, thresholds, model, proxy route, time limit or campaign state while
  the run is active.
- Observe process and campaign status without using partial results to alter
  execution. Any later repaired or matched run requires a new preregistration
  and a new campaign root.
- Record provider, framework, solver, resource or interruption failures as
  observed. They do not authorize restarting the same root.

## Outcome classification

- `CONTINUOUS_OPTIMIZATION_CONFIRMED`: R5 reaches champion v3 or later through
  at least two complete Protocol promotions, and separate independent replays
  support retained improvement over each immediate parent and v1.
- `PROMOTION_CONTINUITY_OBSERVED_REPLAY_PENDING`: R5 completes the required
  promotion path to v3 or later, but independent replay evidence is incomplete.
- `VALID_FUNNEL_BELOW_V3`: the run completes 36 typed stages with valid
  scientific evidence but ends below champion v3.
- `SCIENTIFIC_NO_PROMOTION`: the 36-stage campaign completes with interpretable
  evaluated candidates and no promotion.
- `RUN_INVALID_FRAMEWORK`: the horizon is cut short by framework control flow,
  including recurrence of the R3 patchless-stale stop or failure to continue
  after a typed research rejection.
- `RUN_INVALID_INFRA`: provider, credential, runner, resource or other
  infrastructure failure prevents interpretation of the declared horizon.
- `INTERRUPTED`: an explicit user/system interruption ends the invocation
  before the declared horizon.

An incomplete framework or infrastructure result neither confirms nor refutes
the scientific endpoint. Completed formal stages may be reported as partial
evidence, but they cannot be presented as completion of the pre-registered
36-stage question.
