## Research behavior

The lightweight V3 loop did produce substantive algorithm research. Across
three rotating branches, Terra proposed and usually implemented bounded
ejection repair, exact asymmetric 2-opt deltas, SWAP*, route-cap completion,
and elapsed-budget annealing. Exact expansion reused candidate source without
another H/C. Same-branch H calls used current source and prior formal evidence;
fresh branches still pivoted rather than being forced onto one mechanism.

The successful code interface was not intrinsically too narrow: other C calls
returned complete multi-file edits of roughly 8-10 KB. The one no-op C is
therefore observable incomplete implementation, but the R2 trace does not
record mechanical completion/tool-choice facts needed to distinguish a model
stop from response truncation. Recording provider finish reason, choice/tool
count, selected tool and argument size in the durable trace is sufficient;
none belongs in Decision or creates a new gate.

The final elapsed-budget SA is the strongest research result. The prior solver
cooled against an estimate of 50 iterations/second while observed throughput
was roughly 1.5-2 iterations/second. The candidate instead updated geometric
temperature from elapsed budget across normal, rejected, and repair-error
paths. Relative to its direct parent at the initial screen, worsening annealing
acceptances fell from 375 to 110, latter-half worsening acceptances from 171 to
1, last-quarter worsening acceptances from 74 to 0, and best updates increased
from 79 to 106. Across the 96-pair quality population, late worsening remained
nearly absent and best updates were 315 versus 252 for cached champion runs.
These are mechanism diagnostics, not promotion or runtime claims.

The implementation is not yet a perfect deadline model: its denominator does
not subtract construction, initial VNS, or the tighter outer budget, final
progress typically stops around 0.82-0.96, and the temperature update lags the
current iteration. These are source-grounded next-H facts, not host-mandated
fixes.

SWAP* was also real and active. The quality screen recorded 3,679 strict local
commits, but its 6W/5L/1T cases and wide CI show polarized family response.
Internal move gain did not imply final quality; the X family had the largest
internal gain but no case wins. The fresh route-cap ejection was activated in

the full result was 1W/2L/5T. These are legitimate algorithm negatives, not
framework rejection.

## Measurement reach

R2 was explicitly uncalibrated. It can identify large and broad effects, but it
cannot rule out effects near the practical distance delta 2. The initial 8x4
screen is coarse and serves only as a mechanism router; exact quality evidence
is the 12x8 population.

The SA quality result is broadly non-inferior but not a hidden pass. The fixed
rule required `case wins / all cases >= 0.60`; 6/12 is 0.50, and with twelve
cases even 7/12 would remain below the threshold. Exact ties remained in the
denominator. The candidate met the practical median and CI conditions, had
only one case loss, and all four added population cases were 3W/0L/1T. Changing
to decisive-only 6/7 after seeing the result would be retrospective tuning and
is prohibited.

Repeated common seeds also show limited wall-clock reproducibility: 3/32
paired outcomes improved, 3/32 regressed and 26/32 were unchanged between the
initial and quality executions. The full 12x8 formal result remains valid, but
initial-to-expansion changes cannot all be attributed to added cases or seeds.
R3 must prospectively define a tie-aware case rule, a loss veto, fixed
population/seeds, and a light A/A/null calibration before launch. It must not
reinterpret R2.
