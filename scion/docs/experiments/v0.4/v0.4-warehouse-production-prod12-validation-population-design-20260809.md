`3.2-prod` sets both `validation.n_cases` and `validation.expand_to` to five.
An expanded validation would therefore repeat the same cases and seeds rather
than add statistical evidence. This document fixes the next population before
any new candidate is proposed or evaluated.

`VALID_FUNNEL_FOR_24STAGE_PREREGISTRATION` classification. Its candidate,
objectives and runtime are not used to choose the cases below.

## Allowed preparation checks

Preparation may inspect only properties required to admit a valid problem
input:

- JSON/schema shape and declared metadata;
- unique and complete order identifiers;
- path and split disjointness;
- constructive feasibility of the fixed instance in the declared Warehouse
  model, including capacity and complete locked groups, through the ordinary
  phase-1 and phase-2 oracle semantics.

Preparation must not compare a research candidate or champion, compute a
selection score, inspect a Protocol objective difference, measure solver
runtime, or replace a fixed case based on performance. An in-memory
constructive-feasibility probe of the five fixed specifications completed
before persistence: all five passed both phases; the two cases containing
locked groups had maximum group sizes of 5 and 29 pallets, respectively.

9. new `instance_prod_val_lx150.json`;

The deterministic initial selection remains exactly the five old validation
cases. Expanded validation contains those five plus all five independent new
cases. Protocol `3.3-prod` changes only `validation.expand_to: 5 -> 10` and its
version label; `n_cases`, seeds, thresholds, screening, frozen and canary rules
stay fixed. The general selector must also guarantee
`initial population ⊆ expanded population`; manifest ordering is not a
substitute for that Protocol invariant.

This population repair adds scientific evidence. It adds no identity, digest,
registration, owner, lease, receipt, deployment or build mechanism.
