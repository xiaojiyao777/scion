- `PRODUCTION_PROMOTION_OBSERVED_REPLAY_PENDING`: at least one exact candidate
  completes screening, validation and frozen and receives `PROMOTE`; retention
  still requires independent replay.
- `PRODUCTION_MULTI_PROMOTION_OBSERVED_REPLAY_PENDING`: the uninterrupted run
  reaches at least v3 through two exact promotions; every step and cumulative
  gain still require independent replay.
- `VALID_24STAGE_FUNNEL_HORIZON_CENSORED`: all requested stages complete and an
  exact candidate remains queued for a later formal stage. The root stays
  sealed; the evidence localizes the remaining boundary but is not promotion.
- `VALID_24STAGE_NO_PROMOTION`: all requested stages complete with
  interpretable candidate evidence but no queued funnel or promotion. This is
  failure to demonstrate production improvement under the fixed setup, not a
  claim that no improving Warehouse algorithm exists.
- `RUN_INVALID_INFRA`: the declared horizon is truncated by provider,
  configuration, framework, runner or resource failure.
- `INTERRUPTED`: an explicit external interruption ends the run.
