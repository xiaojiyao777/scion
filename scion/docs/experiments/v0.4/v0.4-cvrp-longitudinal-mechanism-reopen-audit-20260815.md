## Why long rounds are primary evidence

Short screens are useful for activation checks, gross regressions and deciding
whether a predeclared exact candidate should consume more evidence. They are
not sufficient to close a mechanism family or establish a retained effect.

Scion is cumulative. A later source may inherit earlier code, allocator state,
operator order or acceptance behavior. A promising short result can disappear
on expansion, fail a protected case, starve another phase, or belong to the
cumulative branch rather than the most recent edit. The audit therefore gives
the following priority only among records with comparable exact source,
estimand, population, budget and evidence unit:

1. complete frozen/validation evidence on an exact source;
2. complete long screening or quality expansions;
3. immediate-base contrasts that isolate the last edit;
4. complete short screens;
5. activation and semantic diagnostics;
6. implementation-invalid or infrastructure-invalid observations.

Evidence at a lower tier may explain a higher-tier result, but it may not
replace it. An implementation-invalid run is not algorithm-negative; a valid
long-run negative may not later be relabelled an implementation defect without
a concrete, independently identified causal failure. Across different source
ancestry, populations, budgets or estimands, evidence forms a partial order;
longer does not automatically override shorter and isolated does not
automatically override cumulative.

## Classification method

One label cannot express validity, effect and planning disposition at once.
Each exact node is therefore classified on three axes defined in the
[reopen criteria](../../planning/v0.4/v0.4-cvrp-mechanism-reopen-criteria-20260815.md):

- validity: whether the mechanism was actually implemented, active and
  measured, and whether the effect is isolated or cumulative;
- effect: negative, mixed, power-limited, positive-unretained, retained, or
  not estimable;
- disposition: open, conditionally reopenable, or exhausted for an explicit
  scope.

Mechanism identity uses a causal core plus a variant envelope. A change to a
cap, threshold, seed, budget, polling cadence, candidate filter, ordering,
call-site wiring, cache, delta evaluator, telemetry field or case roster is a
direct variant only when reachable transitions, causal mediator, resource
displacement, invariants and the predeclared interaction remain unchanged. If,
for example, a filter changes the neighborhood that can be reached, the causal
core must be recomputed rather than dismissed by its field name.

### The 50-round boundary

The May run completed all 50 rounds and 40 Protocol experiments, with 952
attempted pairs, 951 valid, one candidate-failed pair, 53 wins, 42 losses and
857 ties-or-no-metric rows (the failed pair is included in that final bucket,
so these are not four disjoint counts); it reached no validation and no
promotion. It is
important long-horizon evidence that a wide roster of appended local polish,
route-pair and ruin/recreate proposals did not convert into screening progress.
It is not evidence that every primitive is globally useless: the operators ran
after an already strong ALNS+VNS baseline and were overwhelmingly opportunity-
limited or tie-dominated. The ledger therefore records the concrete nodes and
their placement role; it does not infer broad-family impossibility from the
round count.

## The R3 route-removal decision

The exact cumulative R3 source evaluated after the route-removal round is the
only lead strong enough to outrank another H call, but it remains scientifically
constrained:

- the positive quality stage is complete and may be cited;
- overlapping CPU-heavy repository tests make it
  `OPERATOR_LOAD_CONTAMINATED_FOR_STRICT_PROMOTION_CLAIM` even though all 96
  quality pairs are terminal;
- the 51/52 partial validation is excluded and may not be pooled, resumed or
  selectively finished;
- no Protocol promotion exists;
- the branch is cumulative and the final immediate-base edit was neutral;
- the present decision to prioritize confirmation of this already frozen exact
  source is made after seeing its positive quality outcome;
- the source selection for any future confirmation is therefore outcome-known
  and its discovery is not independent;
- a newly preregistered outcome-blind population, fresh root, isolated host and
  independent operator execution can produce an independent replication of
  the fixed cumulative-source-versus-B0 performance, but cannot make the
  discovery independent or isolate the final route-removal edit.

If the project chooses this path later, it must be a new provider-free exact-
cumulative-source confirmation with fresh roots, fixed source, a new
outcome-blind population, complete stage restart and a separately authorized
solver budget.
It should use zero H and zero C unless an independently necessary source repair
is first identified. A positive confirmation would still have to complete
validation, frozen holdout, Decision and the required fresh B0 retained
comparison under a host-isolation policy frozen before launch. None of those
stages would retroactively make the outcome-selected source an independent
discovery or identify the final edit's causal effect.

## What the continuous process changes

Continuous research does not mean continuously launching the next numbered
round. It means preserving enough ordinary evidence that the next question is
conditioned on the whole history without treating history as a hidden gate.

The immediate sequence is therefore:

1. record this provider-free census and catalog-mapped ledger as ordinary
   planning evidence, not as a gate;
2. keep provider, C and solver work paused;
3. choose explicitly between stopping and an outcome-known R3 cumulative-
   source confirmation;
4. if confirmation is chosen, preregister the exact source, fresh population,
   full funnel, host isolation and claim boundary before any solver call;
5. ask for a new H only after a concrete causal frontier gap survives this
   ledger, and disclose that the H sees a prior-informed exclusion summary.

## Claim boundary

This is a retrospective, outcome-known synthesis of heterogeneous campaigns,
baselines, hosts, populations, budgets and source ancestries. It does not pool
their raw effects, estimate a meta-effect, prove that an entire broad
algorithm family can never work, or create independent confirmation. It closes
only the concrete causal cores and direct variants stated above at their
declared scopes.

No historical artifact was rewritten. No production source, prompt, tool,
gate, hash check, Contract, Verification, Protocol or Decision behavior was
changed. No provider payload was sent and no candidate or solver was run.
