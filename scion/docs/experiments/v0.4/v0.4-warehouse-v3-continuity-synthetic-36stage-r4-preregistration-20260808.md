## Outcome classification

- `CONTINUOUS_OPTIMIZATION_CONFIRMED`: R4 reaches champion v3 or later through
  at least two complete Protocol promotions, and the separate independent
  replays support retained improvement over each immediate parent and v1.
- `PROMOTION_CONTINUITY_OBSERVED_REPLAY_PENDING`: R4 completes the required
  promotion path to v3 or later, but the independent replay evidence is not yet
  complete.
- `VALID_FUNNEL_BELOW_V3`: the run completes 36 typed stages with valid
  scientific evidence but ends below champion v3. Report the exact funnel and
  failure class; do not relabel it as a framework failure.
- `SCIENTIFIC_NO_PROMOTION`: the 36-stage campaign completes with interpretable
  evaluated candidates and no promotion.
- `RUN_INVALID_FRAMEWORK`: the declared horizon is cut short by framework
  control flow, including recurrence of the R3 patchless-stale stop or failure
  to continue after a typed research rejection.
- `RUN_INVALID_INFRA`: provider, runner, resource or other infrastructure
  failure prevents interpretation of the declared horizon.
- `INTERRUPTED`: an explicit user/system interruption ends the invocation
  before the declared horizon.

An incomplete framework or infrastructure result neither confirms nor refutes
the scientific endpoint. Evidence from completed formal stages may be reported
as partial evidence, but it cannot be presented as completion of the
pre-registered 36-stage question.
