## Fixed estimator, routing and Decision rule

The primary statistical unit is a case. Within each case the paired
champion-minus-candidate final `total_distance` effects are reduced by median;
positive means the minimizing candidate is better. With equivalence band zero,
a positive case median is a win, negative is a loss and zero is a tie. Pair
W/L/T is descriptive. `fleet_violation` is the only problem-declared protected
objective and remains a separate regression veto.

Initial screening can never pass directly. It may request the one exact
quality expansion when either:

- case net score `(W-L)/8 >= 0.125`, loss rate `L/8 <= 0.25`, and CI high is
  at least the practical screen delta `2`; or
- it has at least one win, no loss, median delta at least `2`, and CI high at
  least `2`.

Both initial-only expansion routes require zero candidate failed pairs.

The ordinary full case-quality rule may also request the required expansion,
but never promotion, because `require_expanded_for_pass=true`. After that one
expansion, an additional expand result becomes `unclear`; the exact candidate
is not silently given new cases or seeds.

Quality, validation and frozen each use the complete 12-case block and require:

- case net score `(W-L)/12 >= 0.25`;
- case loss rate `L/12 <= 0.20`;
- median distance improvement at least `2` for quality and `1` thereafter;
- bootstrap median CI low `>= 0`;
- no protected fleet regression.

The 10-second canary runs before every formal evaluation and hard-vetoes the
candidate on failure. Case-median confidence intervals are deterministic 95%
intervals using 1,000 bootstrap samples and seed `42`: the screening execution
default and the explicit validation setting that also applies to frozen are
both 1,000 at the executable commit.

Configured `win_rate_min` values remain compatibility fallback for protocols
without the atomic net/loss fields. They are not an additional R3 gate. The
descriptive decisive-case count is not a threshold. Validation declares all 12
cases initially and `expand_to=12`, so uncertainty cannot create a duplicate
population; it fails conservatively when the fixed evidence is insufficient.

Runtime is problem-owned by instance dimension for every formal stage and
final replay: through dimension 100 use 30 seconds; 101-200 use 45; 201-350 use
60; 351-700 use 90; and 701-1001 use 120. Canary uses 10 seconds. `CMT3/CMT4`
use fixed 45-second aliases; `CMT2` uses the 30-second default. The CLI 30
seconds is fallback only. Runtime or solver failure cannot be converted to a
numeric distance improvement.

Contract rejection or Verification rejection records one typed research
outcome and schedules a fresh H from that branch's last verified clean or
provisional source, or champion when the branch has none; the failed call is
never retried. Missing provider terminal response, local context/binding
failure, workspace/infrastructure/resource failure or interruption is
terminal. The campaign is never resumed. If stage 16 leaves an exact candidate
queued for a fixed next stage, seal it as horizon-censored rather than
extending the run.

## Outcome classifications

Classification precedence is promotion; stage-16 queued-candidate censoring;
stage-16 completed no-promotion; a pre-horizon non-evaluated terminal
infrastructure outcome; then an explicit external-interruption subtype.

- `CVRP_PROMOTION_OBSERVED_REPLAY_PENDING`: an exact candidate passes quality,
  validation and frozen and deterministic Decision promotes it; retained B0
  improvement remains pending the predeclared independent replay.
- `VALID_16STAGE_FUNNEL_HORIZON_CENSORED`: 16 formal stages complete with an
  exact candidate queued for another fixed stage.
- `VALID_16STAGE_NO_PROMOTION`: 16 interpretable stages complete with no
  promotion and no queued candidate. This rejects only the exact evaluated
  candidates at this measurement rung.
- `RUN_INVALID_INFRA`: provider transport, configuration, framework harness,
  shared/champion evidence acquisition or resource failure truncates the
  horizon. A candidate-attributable solver/runtime failure that completes a
  formal evaluation remains evaluated negative evidence, not root-level infra.
- `INTERRUPTED_RESEARCH_REJECTION_STALL`: an external interruption seals a root
  whose formal count cannot advance through repeated candidate-local research
  rejections. It is not a solver-performance negative.
- `INTERRUPTED`: another explicit external interruption ends the run.

No outcome may be reclassified by changing source, cases, seeds, thresholds,
time budgets or context after launch.
