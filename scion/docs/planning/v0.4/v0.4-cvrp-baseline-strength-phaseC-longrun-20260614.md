# CVRP Baseline-Strength Phase C Long-Run Follow-Up

Status: pre-registered design, not a postrun conclusion.

## Purpose

Phase B showed that CVRP was not simply "unable to improve". It produced
measurable pair-level signal under the ALNS-only research surface, but the
8-round budget censored the strongest branch at `queue_validate`. Phase C tests
the repaired v0.4 framework behavior: staged diagnostic validation, bounded
post-budget validation/frozen drain, and longer branch budget.

This is still a v3-aligned experiment. Contract, Verification, Protocol,
`DecisionFeatures`, and deterministic Decision remain the formal boundary. MDE,
BKS gap, VNS telemetry, prompt/context inspection, branch depth, and mechanism
family analysis are postrun research diagnostics unless explicitly encoded in
problem-owned config and surfaced through deterministic protocol features.

## Hypotheses

1. With 16 effective rounds plus bounded stage-transition drain, at least one
   CVRP branch that would have been censored at 8 rounds can enter validation or
   frozen testing when it has enough deterministic protocol signal.
2. ALNS-only remains a weaker but more measurable CVRP research surface; it may
   show more validation reach than ALNS+VNS, but any result must be interpreted
   against the ALNS-only MDE and weaker BKS gap.
3. ALNS+VNS remains the canonical CVRP baseline surface. A weak or null result
   there is evidence about the current research difficulty, not evidence that
   ALNS-only should replace the canonical baseline.
4. The repaired `stage_transition_drain` should execute pending validation or
   frozen work after the 16 effective proposal/screening rounds without
   incrementing `effective_rounds_completed` or `proposal_attempts_consumed`.

- ALNS+VNS results interpreted against ALNS+VNS MDE and BKS gap

  failures, and stale-source signals
- prompt/context: compact research signals and branch lessons are not silently
  truncated; code-stage target/current/champion source remains visible

- Do not tune validation/frozen gates based on postrun diagnostics from this
  same run.
- Do not feed MDE, BKS gap, prompt text, or raw telemetry directly into
  deterministic Decision.
- Do not use WSL/server runtime differences as formal quality evidence.
