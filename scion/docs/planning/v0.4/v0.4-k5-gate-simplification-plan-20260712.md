# Scion v0.4 K5 Gate Simplification Plan

*Date: 2026-07-12*
*Status: accepted design; K5.5 implementation moved before remaining K4.3 producer cleanup*

## Objective

Make Scion test grounded algorithmic ideas without first teaching the model
Scion's branch-governance dialect. Preserve identity, safety, correctness, and
formal scientific protocol, while replacing duplicate quality gates and
heuristic termination with host-derived audit and scheduler signals.

This plan does not set, tune, or introduce budgets or truncation. Existing
hidden controls are removal targets. Formal experiments remain locked until K4
and K5 acceptance pass on the final integrated worktree.

## Architectural Decision

```text
provider response
  -> parse + safe ownership + exact target/hypothesis binding
  -> host-derived GovernanceEnvelope (audit only)
  -> one authoritative HypothesisContractDecision
  -> code
  -> one authoritative PatchContractDecision / Verification
  -> Protocol
  -> formal Decision
  -> scheduler consumes hints but cannot overwrite Decision
```

Each fact and decision has one owner:

- provider response owns research intent and proposed code;
- the host owns branch history, novelty comparison, lesson availability,
  telemetry paths, and governance metadata;
- Explore Contract owns the formal pre-experiment hypothesis/patch decision;
- Verification owns executable correctness;
- Protocol owns formal comparative evidence;
- Decision owns continue/repair/promote/reject outcome;
- scheduler owns priority and branch selection only.

## Typed Boundaries

### `ResearchHypothesis`

Provider-owned minimum:

- `hypothesis_text` and expected algorithmic effect;
- problem-owned `change_locus`;
- `action` and conditionally required `target_file`;
- `predicted_direction`, `target_weakness`, and `expected_effect`;
- optional `suggested_weight` only where the problem surface supports it.

The provider does not have to emit branch lessons, clean-fork claims,
material-difference proof objects, protected-case declarations, Scion reason
codes, objective-governance prose, complexity/runtime-budget claims,
telemetry declarations, novelty signatures, or mechanism-id governance.

### `GovernanceEnvelope`

Host-derived, immutable, and excluded from provider obligations:

- exact target/source owner and approved-hypothesis binding;
- prior branch mechanisms and exact duplicate digests;
- available branch lessons and follow-up relationships;
- host-computed novelty/material-difference diagnostics;
- canonical telemetry paths and attribution requirements;
- scheduler hints and provenance.

Missing model governance fields cannot make this envelope invalid. The host
either derives the fact, marks it unknown, or records an audit diagnostic.

### `ContractDecision`

One formal owner returns:

- `allow`;
- `repairable` with typed executable/safety reason;
- `reject` with typed executable/safety reason;
- audit diagnostics that never change the status.

There is no second inner/outer quality status with competing authority.

### `ExecutionOutcome`

Durable states distinguish:

- `research_rejected`;
- `not_evaluated`;
- `blocked_infra`;
- `resource_exhausted`;
- `interrupted`;
- `evaluated`.

`blocked_infra`, `resource_exhausted`, `not_evaluated`, and `interrupted` are
never converted to `ABANDON` or a negative research result. A
`research_rejected` outcome may map to explicit rejection only when the single
authoritative Contract/Decision records a typed reason and provenance.

## What Remains Hard

- safe primitive response parsing and required research-target fields;
- authoritative context, receipt, trace, lineage, and attempt identity;
- explicit user-forced target and problem-owned surface/path/action/editable/
  frozen/import/API permissions;
- exact approved hypothesis and target-source binding;
- typed edits, AST, interface, full integration-source visibility;
- syntax, tests, feasibility, solution consistency, objective recomputation,
  nondeterminism, candidate crash, and process runaway;
- screening/validation/frozen isolation and comparability;
- validation/frozen pair completeness, thresholds, CI, positive-only policy,
  and formal promotion attribution;
- screening candidate-failure veto and champion-failure
  unclear/inconclusive semantics, without a universal completeness gate;
- atomic promotion prepare/commit/recovery;
- authentication, balance exhaustion, interrupt, process watchdog, and durable
  persistence failure.

## What Becomes Audit or Scheduling

- material-difference, causal-path, branch-lesson, clean-fork, protected-case,
  and algorithmic-intervention document shapes;
- novelty signatures, including exact-duplicate diagnostics;
- target-intent mechanism-name agreement after file/source grounding succeeds;
- repair-first, same-mechanism, and weak-positive prose rules;
- model-declared telemetry identity when canonical paths are host-known;
- static complexity heuristics short of a demonstrably unbounded execution
  path;
- ordinary comparative slowdown;
- screening-stage telemetry activation/effect absence;
- repeated quality/Contract signatures and outcome streaks;
- self-reported `test_hint` wording.

## Migration Slices

### Sequencing update

K4.4 SourceLedger is accepted, but the remaining K4.3 feedback/runtime producer
cleanup cannot stay structurally small while historical agentic compact bytes
remain a hard compatibility target. K5.5 first removes the obsolete
agentic source/output/read-count projections and establishes full-context
parity. The V3/K6 audit then retires the entire agentic runtime instead of
repairing every remaining session-budget and retry branch in place. K4.3-A
through D can delete their `lossless` branches against the sole direct
full-context contract instead of building ledgers and renderers solely to
reproduce behavior scheduled for physical deletion.

This is a sequencing change only. The final run lock still requires all K4 and
K5 acceptance on the integrated worktree, and no experiment may launch from the
transition state.

### K5.0 Characterize and freeze authority

- freeze current warehouse `direct_v3` and CVRP `agentic_ablation`
  allow/reject fixtures;
- record current inner, pipeline, and Explore decisions separately;
- label successor55 evidence with launch commit `ff184608`;
- assert current checkout behavior independently of the old artifact;
- map every rejection reason to safety, correctness, formal protocol, audit, or
  scheduler ownership.

### K5.1 Host-project governance

- introduce `GovernanceEnvelope` from authoritative branch/evidence state;
- restore the V3 hypothesis response surface and remove host-known governance
  fields from the provider schema and prompt entirely;
- convert material-difference, branch-lesson, novelty, and expected-telemetry
  shape failures to audit diagnostics;
- keep exact-duplicate evidence canonical, durable, and visible to scheduler
  and Contract diagnostics without letting it alone change Contract status;
- keep target/source ownership and identity/provenance collision evidence
  typed and fail-closed;
- prove model omission cannot prevent code generation.

`GovernanceEnvelope` belongs beside `SafeProposalInputs` and
`ProposalContextSnapshot`; do not create another registry or generic helper.
Its digest participates in snapshot/attempt identity, but
`provider_context()` never exposes the envelope itself.

### K5.2 Establish one Contract owner

- make novelty/governance portions of agentic semantic checks diagnostic;
- route explicit boundary, objective-policy, and ownership contradictions to
  the authoritative Contract and keep them fail-closed;
- make preview/smoke advisory only after the outer Contract/Verification proves
  it executes the equivalent hard checks;
- remove duplicated problem-quality decisions from inner agentic validation and
  `ProposalPipeline` where Explore already owns the Contract;
- retain one hypothesis Contract before code approval and one patch
  Contract/Verification before evaluation;
- preserve durable diagnostics from all advisory layers.

`ProposalPipeline` becomes parse/bind/receipt/attempt publication only. Remove
its forced/boundary, repair-first, weak-positive, and adapter problem-quality
formal rejections once the equivalent hard structural checks are owned by the
outer Contract. Delete Explore's separate material-difference and
branch-lesson pre-code blockers. Move the real CVRP unsupported integration API
check into its problem-owned outer C9e Contract provider; do not retain an
adapter patch-quality duplicate.

### K5.3 Make circuit infra-only

- define typed provider/transport/infra fault categories;
- remove forced-boundary, novelty, quality, Contract, and lifecycle rejections
  from circuit failure accounting;
- keep balance/auth/transport/provider/trace persistence failure visible;
- remove automatic same-prompt provider/format/timeout retry loops; every real
  provider call is one durable attempt rather than a hidden repeat;
- replace indefinite foreground 429 waiting with durable blocked-infra state and
  explicit operator cancellation/resume.

### K5.4 Restore Decision sovereignty

- prevent generic `BranchLifecyclePolicy` from replacing a formal Decision;
- convert no-effect, marginal, diagnostic, runtime-loss, same-family, and
  repeated-quality streaks into scheduler hints;
- remove derived hard terminators that turn research heuristics into lifecycle
  outcomes;
- retain only requested experiment envelope and a scheduler no-progress
  watchdog for actual orchestration defects.

### K5.5 Remove hidden agentic controls

- remove session tool-step, tool-call, code-call, observation-character,
  repeated-call, and wall-time budgets from the research path rather than
  treating a configured zero as a special unlimited mode;
- delete budget-reserve, compact-to-fit, omit-to-fit, and
  `RESULT_TOO_LARGE` branches that can replace a real observation with a
  smaller or synthetic one;
- remove fixed source character projections and file/read counts from the
  production path;
- move OpenAI agentic calls to provider-managed output and remove truncation
  retry;
- remove pre-code wall-time reserve and fixed semantic/preview/grounding/code
  repair counts as terminal research controls;
- retain typed provider/transport failure evidence, explicit operator
  cancellation, path/exposure safety, and process-runaway protection; an
  orchestration no-progress detector may diagnose an actual scheduler defect
  but cannot manufacture a research result;
- keep agentic mode classified as ablation until these controls are gone and
  parity is re-established.

Implementation order:

1. `K5.5a` — OpenAI-compatible provider-managed output and deletion of
   truncation retry: implemented and baseline-green.
2. `K5.5b` — full source/read projection and deletion of source character and
   file-count controls: in progress.
3. `K5.5c-1` — delete the pre-code/preview wall-time reserve: implemented;
   integrated tests wait for K5.5b to stabilize.
4. The remaining session tool/call/observation/wall-time controls,
   compact-to-fit branches, and semantic/schema/grounding/preview/code-repair
   loops are K6 physical-deletion predicates. Do not create another in-place
   compatibility layer merely to remove them one by one.

### K5.6 Separate outcome states

- propagate infra, interruption, resource exhaustion, not-evaluated, and
  research rejection distinctly through campaign status, StepRecord, postrun,
  handoff, and acceptance;
- forbid evaluation exception fallback from manufacturing `ABANDON`;
- forbid frozen resource exhaustion from manufacturing a negative result;
- prove resumption does not reuse tainted pending state.

## Acceptance

### Research path

- Both audited proposal paths—warehouse `direct_v3` and CVRP
  `agentic_ablation`—reach code without branch-governance dialect fields.
- Exact target/file/source ownership remains fail-closed.
- Missing novelty/material/lesson/telemetry declarations create audit records,
  not proposal rejection.
- Exact-duplicate evidence remains canonical, durable, and visible to scheduler
  and Contract diagnostics, but does not alone change Contract status.

### Single authority

- every formal hypothesis rejection comes from one Contract decision;
- every formal patch rejection comes from one Contract/Verification decision;
- preview/self-check diagnostics cannot contradict or duplicate the terminal
  status;
- all advisory diagnostics remain durable and attributable.

### Circuit and lifecycle

- three research-quality rejections do not trip the provider circuit;
- provider/transport failures do affect the infra circuit;
- formal `CONTINUE`/`REPAIR` cannot become `ABANDON`, park, or archive through a
  generic heuristic;
- scheduler hints affect priority only.

### Evidence semantics

- `blocked_infra`, `resource_exhausted`, `not_evaluated`, `interrupted`, and
  `research_rejected` remain distinct through postrun acceptance;
- no missing evaluation is scored as a solver-negative result;
- Decision and promotion still require complete formal evidence.

### No-interference rule

- production proposal context and provider output have no Scion-imposed hidden
  truncation or fixed item/file/read count; provider-native limits are explicit
  transport facts;
- production agentic execution has no Scion-imposed tool-step, tool-call,
  observation-character, retry-count, or session-wall-time research budget;
- no new budget, cap, summary substitution, or truncation is introduced;
- no formal experiment launches from a dirty or transition worktree.
