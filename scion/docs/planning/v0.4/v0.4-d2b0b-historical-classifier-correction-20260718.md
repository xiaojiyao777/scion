## Decision

The attempted executable historical classifier, its v2 inventory schema, and
its scanner/verifier prototypes are rejected and removed. They are not an
implementation plan, activation contract, v0.4 prerequisite, or authority
source.

The immutable v1 evidence remains:

- historical activation addendum SHA-256:
  `a9d912ff122883fd75ead5c9e04a787ce1245a17c75900281a445b2b0ee2766c`;
- v1 inventory schema SHA-256:
  `e2d5b7cde488fded97d084b209b7a17343af6ee6157a89091bcdbfa488def503`.

Those v1 artifacts preserve the earlier review record, but their semantic
classifier is not closed. No v1 scanner output can authorize migration,
activation, adoption, selection, receipt publication, or active reopen.

## Why v2 was attempted

Implementation and independent-verifier work found that v1 froze artifact
shapes and many codecs but did not fully freeze accepted legacy schema
families, literal role decoders, total reason predicates, primary-identity
semantics, condition-count units, D1 bindings, and cross-record invariants.
The v2 attempt tried to make those rules independently executable from one
machine-readable contract rather than letting scanner and verifier infer them
from production code or from each other.

## Fixed-hash review result

The reviewed v2 candidate had SHA-256
`4e8fd2cead3c72f39fd0464369debddb8bc346a81fdefd13d4834de772eb456a`.
Independent review reported P0=0 and rejected it on unresolved P1 contract
defects, including:

1. schema-shape hashing was incorrectly modeled as a structured domain digest
   instead of raw canonical-shape JSON SHA-256;
2. transition-group hashing retained the v1 domain and omitted the v2 path,
   phase, and complete accepted START/generated row identities;
3. all 36 reason predicates, condition counts, and cross-record invariants
   remained opaque names rather than total executable semantics;
4. D1 intent/event bindings did not close all physical row, transaction, and
   intent-reference relationships;
5. presence-bitmap ordering was not a total typed-identity ordering;
6. generated tainted-artifact arrays did not freeze exact length three and
   index bindings;
7. nested required opaque strings still admitted empty or boundary-whitespace
   values;
8. tests derived key matrices from the schema instead of freezing independent
   family, locator, digest, binding, ordering, and negative constants; and
9. an unreachable `git_commit` definition remained.

Trying to repair these findings expanded the contract toward an approximately
747 KB JSON DSL and a second interpreter for filesystem, SQLite, decoder,
join, digest, predicate, and aggregation algorithms. The DSL still required
helper semantics and therefore did not deliver genuine scanner/verifier
independence. This design direction is rejected, not deferred.

## Accepted replacement

The final v0.4 boundary is the fresh-only design:

- document: `v0.4-d2b0b-fresh-only-activation-boundary-20260718.md`;
- SHA-256:
  `ba7a72e2eeb2c6304718224c73b98fc9204e77b72d953a1e631601388f4be400`.

V0.4 may bootstrap only one explicitly targeted campaign database proved
stably absent. Every present database and every historical corpus scan is
non-authoritative and read-only. Historical corpus diagnostics may continue
later as ordinary tooling or compatibility research, but they are nonblocking
for current algorithm research and cannot produce an activation selection,
receipt, adoption, migration, or reopen authority chain.

There is no historical selection receipt and no legacy adoption path in the
accepted v0.4 design.
