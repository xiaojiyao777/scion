## 1. Purpose

The manager/lifecycle correction requires H11 to freeze absence of every
accepted terminal/final output before an external operator may authorize the
run-main release.  The first installer candidate validated only the entries
that `PERMIT_READY.absent_paths` chose to list.  A READY receipt could omit one
accepted output, leave that omitted output present, and still obtain a permit.

This is one protocol-closure defect, not a local missing conditional.  H11
needs an independently derivable, closed absence authority shared by the
harness, external authorizer and final ledger.

This correction refines section 4 of
`v0.4-w3-generic-formal-fixture-manager-lifecycle-correction-design-20260719.md`.
All other constraints in that design remain normative.

## 2. Authority ownership

`PERMIT_READY` is evidence, not policy authority.  Neither the authorization
manifest nor `PERMIT_READY` may choose the roles or paths whose absence is
sufficient.

The closed authority has two independent inputs:

1. the H11 `ScenarioPolicy`, frozen in harness source, owns the exact accepted
   output-role set;
2. the retained execution-manifest source owns the one path assigned to every
   role and the formal-root authority from which the absent frozen-root path
   is derived.

The external authorizer parses the retained execution-manifest bytes and
derives the same inventory itself.  It does not import an inventory from
READY, an authorization manifest or a caller argument.

The exact H11 manifest output roles are:

```text
closer-properties
exec-stop-post-properties
final
final-closer-properties
final-run-properties
h0
h12-absence
journal
manager-events
run-main-properties
signals
source-selector
```

The absent final-freeze namespace contributes one additional role:

```text
frozen-root = <formal-root>/frozen
```

Those thirteen authorities form one closed coverage universe, but they are not
all absent at the permit boundary.  `ScenarioPolicy` partitions them into two
disjoint, exact sets:

```text
pre-permit present prerequisites:
  h0
  run-main-properties

pre-release absent future outputs:
  closer-properties
  exec-stop-post-properties
  final
  final-closer-properties
  final-run-properties
  frozen-root
  h12-absence
  journal
  manager-events
  signals
  source-selector
```

Their disjoint union must equal the twelve H11 manifest outputs plus the
derived frozen-root authority.  The two present prerequisites are published and
retained at their already-required lifecycle points: H0 before StartUnit, and
run-main properties while accepting the post-Start run acquisition.  The
eleven future outputs remain absent until after the operator permit is
consumed.

Every manifest output path remains an immediate child of its retained
`input_root` or `receipt_root`; the frozen-root path has the one layout above.
Each ordered inventory is sorted by role.  Duplicate roles, duplicate paths,
missing roles, extra roles, reordering, a path outside its allowed parent or
an alias with any H11 transaction path is invalid.

## 3. Exact H11 transaction layout

The retained formal root defines this sole transaction namespace, including
three deliberately named poison-on-failure staging authorities:

```text
<formal-root>/authority/harness/H11/AUTHORIZE-RELEASE.json
<formal-root>/authority/harness/H11/PERMIT_READY.pending
<formal-root>/authority/harness/H11/PERMIT_READY.json
<formal-root>/authority/harness/H11/PERMIT.pending
<formal-root>/authority/harness/H11/PERMIT.json
<formal-root>/authority/harness/H11/PERMIT-LEDGER.pending
<formal-root>/authority/harness/H11/PERMIT-LEDGER.json
```

Two root-only commit FIFOs are precreated by the tree installer and are not
transaction files:

```text
<formal-root>/fifo/h11-ready-committed.fifo
<formal-root>/fifo/h11-permit-committed.fifo
```

They are distinct root:root `0600` FIFOs with retained path/device/inode/mode/
uid/gid references.  Their exact one-write payloads are respectively
`SCION_H11_READY_COMMITTED_V1\n` and
`SCION_H11_PERMIT_COMMITTED_V1\n`.  No other writer or reader is authorized.

The prepare-tree manifest's FIFO row has exact keys `role, path, owner`.
Ordinary acquisition rows use `owner=fixture`; the two reserved roles
`h11-ready-commit` and `h11-permit-commit` use `owner=root` and the exact paths
above.  The tree/preflight receipts repeat role/path/owner/uid/gid/mode/device/
inode.  Missing, extra or filename-inferred ownership is invalid.

Before StartUnit the harness pins both FIFO identities with
`O_PATH|O_NOFOLLOW|O_CLOEXEC`.  Endpoint opens use the pinned identity path,
blocking flags and an immediate fstat identity proof; O_NONBLOCK is forbidden.
For READY the authorizer is the `O_RDONLY|O_CLOEXEC` endpoint and the harness
is the `O_WRONLY|O_CLOEXEC` endpoint; for PERMIT those directions reverse.
Either endpoint may reach its blocking open first.  Safety depends only on the
identity-proved rendezvous, never scheduler order.  Each writer performs one
PIPE_BUF-sized exact write and closes.  Each reader reads until EOF and
requires exactly the one payload, with no prefix, suffix or second frame.

All seven paths must equal these paths exactly.  Merely being relative to the
formal root is insufficient.  Their parent is one retained root:root `0700`
directory authority.  The H11 output roots are also exact:

```text
input_root   = <formal-root>/input                              root:root 0555
receipt_root = <formal-root>/authority/harness/H11/receipts     root:root 0555
```

The formal root, authority, harness, H11, input, receipt and fifo directory
chain is opened with nofollow/openat, retained by descriptor, and frozen in
one ordered `directory_chain` with exact role/path/device/inode/mode/uid/gid
objects.  Its exact order and modes are:

```text
formal-root     <formal-root>                                  0711
authority-root  <formal-root>/authority                        0700
harness-root    <formal-root>/authority/harness                0700
scenario-root   <formal-root>/authority/harness/H11            0700
input-root      <formal-root>/input                            0555
receipt-root    <formal-root>/authority/harness/H11/receipts   0555
fifo-root       <formal-root>/fifo                             0711
```

All are root:root and distinct inodes.  `frozen-root` must not exist; its
negative authority is the retained formal-root descriptor plus a watch on the
single child name `frozen`.  This preserves the final-freeze transaction,
which later creates the entire subtree including FROZEN, SHA256SUMS and the
frozen H11 ledger.  All transaction, coverage, commit-FIFO, acquisition and
authority-source paths are globally non-aliased canonically.  Every present
directory, source, FIFO or transaction object has a unique `(device,inode)`;
hardlink aliases are invalid.  An absent path acquires an inode constraint
only if it becomes the one allowed object at its declared later phase.

H11 does not stretch the ordinary single-leaf `CreationWatch` into a directory
ledger.  It adds one in-file `AuthorityDirectoryWatch` per retained
formal-root/input-root/receipt-root/scenario-root descriptor.  Each inotify
watch is installed through `/proc/self/fd/<retained-dirfd>`, records its watch
descriptor to the exact `directory_chain` role, and listens only for CREATE,
MOVED_FROM, MOVED_TO and DELETE plus fatal DELETE_SELF/overflow/ignored/unmount
masks.  IN_MOVE_SELF is also fatal so a move-away/move-back cannot erase
directory history.  The parent role is exactly one of `formal-root`, `input-root`,
`receipt-root`, `scenario-root`.  The closed
watched-name map is derived from the manifest outputs, seven transaction names
and `frozen`; an event for any other new name rejects.  A leaf that remains
present is opened nofollow relative to the retained parent.  For READY and
LEDGER the harness-owned retained publication FD supplies the staging identity.
For externally published PERMIT, the harness may drain events only after the
pending name has disappeared; it therefore binds the exact contiguous pending
CREATE, pending MOVED_FROM and final MOVED_TO sequence retrospectively to the
pinned final permit full reference.  The latter two rows share one nonzero
cookie, all three rows carry the same device/inode, and no intervening DELETE,
second CREATE or other name is allowed.  No event authority requires reopening
a parent by path or assumes the watcher ran before rename.

The H11-only `permit_authority` object in the execution manifest has the exact
keys:

```text
schema = scion.generic_backend.h11_operator_permit_authority.v1
scenario
run_unit
permit_path
permit_parent
permit_ready_path
permit_ledger_path
permit_ready_staging_path
permit_staging_path
permit_ledger_staging_path
directory_chain
ready_commit_fifo
permit_commit_fifo
present_prerequisite_roles
future_absence_inventory
```

`directory_chain` is the exact seven-object array above.  Each commit FIFO is
one full path/device/inode/mode/uid/gid reference.
`present_prerequisite_roles` is exactly the two-role array above.
`future_absence_inventory` is the canonical eleven-item inventory derived
above, not an independent declaration: the harness and authorizer each
recompute it and require exact ordered equality.  Non-H11 manifests reject
`permit_authority`.

## 4. Publication and authorization chronology

Before `StartUnit`, the harness must:

1. retain the execution-manifest source and all static authorities;
2. retain the transaction/output parents, formal-root negative authority,
   commit FIFO identities and watches for every future-output/transaction
   final name plus the `frozen` child;
3. prove all transaction paths, all twelve manifest outputs and frozen-root are
   initially absent;
4. publish H0 no-replace, retain its full reference, and reject any event or
   path inconsistent with that sole present prerequisite;
5. reject pre-Start ARMED creation under the ordinary acquisition watch
   contract.

After the run-main acquisition and ARMED binding are accepted, the harness
publishes and retains the run-main-properties reference.  It recomputes the
closed partition from its policy and retained manifest, proves the two exact
present references and eleven exact absences, and publishes one
`scion.generic_backend.h11_permit_ready.v1`.  READY carries
`present_outputs`, the complete ordered `absent_paths`, and the exact permit
authority.  Each present reference has exact role/path/SHA-256/device/inode/
mode/uid/gid.  No release occurs.

READY, permit and ledger use one shared named-staging publication primitive
with this exact order:

```text
openat(retained-parent-fd, staging-name,
       O_RDWR|O_CREAT|O_EXCL|O_NOFOLLOW|O_CLOEXEC) retained staging FD
-> write all canonical bytes
-> fchmod 0444
-> lseek/read back and fstat/revalidate bytes/identity/mode/owner
-> fsync the still-retained staging FD
-> renameat2(retained-parent-fd, staging-name,
             retained-parent-fd, final-name, RENAME_NOREPLACE)
   while retaining that FD
-> fsync the retained parent FD
-> retained-parent nofollow stat proves final name is that same inode
-> form the final full reference from the same retained inode
```

Only leaf names are accepted by the primitive; lexical parent paths and
path-based open/rename are forbidden.  No reopen occurs inside the primitive.
The FD remains retained through the
publication's commit handshake or ledger barrier.  Metadata changes after the
file fsync are forbidden.

READY publication applies that primitive to root:root
`PERMIT_READY.pending` and `PERMIT_READY.json`.  Only successful
completion permits it to open the ready-commit FIFO writer and emit the one
exact READY_COMMITTED payload.  The external authorizer's retained reader
endpoint rendezvous blocks without timeout until that byte; either endpoint's
blocking open may be scheduled first.  A failure before
rename may leave the staging path; a failure after rename may leave the final
path; neither emits the commit byte, so neither can advance the transaction.

After READY MOVED_TO is visible, the operator may publish and fsync the exact
root:root `0444` AUTH source no-replace and invoke the one-shot authorizer.
AUTH publication occurs before the READY_COMMITTED byte but can advance only
because that byte later proves the READY parent fsync succeeded.

The root authorizer opens the authorization manifest, harness manifest, READY
and ARMED once with nofollow, pins the complete directory/FIFO authority, and
retains all descriptors.  It then opens the validated ready-commit FIFO reader
and blocks for the exact READY_COMMITTED payload.  After consuming the byte it
revalidates every retained source, directory and FIFO before its first
mutation.  It must:

1. validate the exact transaction layout and all retained directory
   references;
2. validate the harness manifest's H11 schema, scenario, run unit, exact
   output roots, exact twelve output roles and paths;
3. independently derive the two-role present and eleven-role absent sets and
   prove their closed union;
4. require exact ordered equality among the derived sets, permit authority,
   READY `present_outputs` and READY `absent_paths`;
5. open once and retain/revalidate the two present output sources;
6. validate the exact phase state of every transaction path; the harness
   separately owns and later validates the retained watch histories;
7. revalidate all retained sources and directory identities.  The harness,
   not the authorizer, remains owner of the pre-Start creation watches.

The authorizer then performs one final mutation-adjacent barrier: from the
retained directory descriptors it revalidates every future absence, all
required staging/final absences and the frozen-root absence.  This barrier is
the last authority operation before the O_EXCL staging create; no hook,
lookup, caller callback or other side effect is permitted between them.

Only then may it apply the shared primitive to root:root `0444`
`PERMIT.pending` and `PERMIT.json`.  The PERMIT creation watch therefore
cannot observe a partially written final file.  Only after the retained parent
fsync succeeds may the authorizer open
the permit-commit FIFO writer and emit the one exact PERMIT_COMMITTED payload.
A pre-rename failure may leave staging; a post-rename failure may leave final;
both are poisoned, emit no commit byte and are never cleaned or retried.

The permit keeps the complete READY source reference and adds
`present_outputs_sha256` and `future_absence_sha256`.  These are SHA-256 over
the respective exact canonical arrays.  Canonical JSON means the existing
shared ABI: `ensure_ascii=True`, `allow_nan=False`, `sort_keys=True`, compact
separators `(',', ':')`, one trailing newline, then ASCII bytes.  A subset
digest, caller-selected inventory or lexical-only ledger path is invalid.

The harness blocks on the retained permit-commit FIFO reader and consumes one
exact PERMIT_COMMITTED payload before it may consume the permit.  It accepts
the permit only if it derives the same partition again,
the READY full reference matches, both digests match the canonical bytes and
the two present sources still match their full references.  After pinning the
permit and before any ledger or release write, it revalidates from retained
parent descriptors that every future path is absent, the exact transaction
phase holds and no forbidden future-name creation/move event occurred.

It then applies the shared primitive to root:root `0444`
`PERMIT-LEDGER.pending`/`PERMIT-LEDGER.json`, but does not release yet.  The
ledger carries the full permit
authority, full path/SHA-256/device/inode/mode/uid/gid references for the
authorization manifest, READY, permit and both present outputs, plus both
canonical inventory digests.

After the ledger and parent fsync, the harness opens the ordinary run-main
release writer through its pre-Start O_PATH identity and proves the FIFO by
fstat.  It then performs a second and final release-adjacent barrier from the
retained directory descriptors and watches.
It requires the exact ledger-committed transaction phase, every future output
including frozen-root still absent, no overflow/ignored/unmount watch event,
and no create/move event for a future name.  One `os.write` on the already-open
writer is the immediately following operation.  No open, identity prove,
callback or filesystem lookup is permitted between barrier and write.  The
in-memory barrier receipt is later
embedded in terminal `final`; a crash before that final evidence causes
conservative non-acceptance.

The H11 terminal `final` receipt embeds the ledger's complete reference,
digest, `directory_chain` and final release-barrier receipt.  The final-freeze
process cannot inherit earlier descriptors.  It independently re-pins the
exact directory chain with nofollow/openat, requires equality to `final`,
reopens the full `execution_manifest_source` reference carried by `final`,
parses its exact H11 permit authority, requires equality to the ledger and
release objects, derives the ledger path only from that authority/chain, and
reopens/revalidates the full ledger reference.  It copies the ledger as the
special frozen role `h11-permit-ledger` and cross-binds its
source/destination in the freeze receipt.  H11 final-freeze output order is
exactly:

```text
[install-receipt,
 *sorted(required_outputs - {'final'}),
 h11-permit-ledger,
 harness-final]
```

The generic fixture freeze-manifest output row is upgraded uniformly to exact
`role, path, sha256, device, inode, mode, uid, gid`; the legacy shorter row is
invalid for every role.  Its top-level `install_receipt` and
`harness_receipt` references use the same seven reference fields and equal the
corresponding `install-receipt` and `harness-final` output rows.  The freeze
receipt carries the same full source
reference and the full frozen-destination reference.  For
`h11-permit-ledger`, the caller must repeat the exact full source reference
derived above; the installer compares that row for exact equality before
pinning and never treats it as path selection.  A caller therefore cannot
override the ledger path or identity without rejection.  A terminal/
final-freeze result that lacks this chain is invalid.

### 4.4 Exact transaction state and receipt ABI

Every phase validates all seven transaction paths, not only the next
destination:

| phase | AUTH | READY.pending | READY | PERMIT.pending | PERMIT | LEDGER.pending | LEDGER |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| pre-Start | absent | absent | absent | absent | absent | absent | absent |
| READY visible | absent | absent | present | absent | absent | absent | absent |
| authorizer input / READY_COMMITTED handoff | present | absent | present | absent | absent | absent | absent |
| PERMIT committed | present | absent | present | absent | present | absent | absent |
| LEDGER committed / release | present | absent | present | absent | present | absent | present |

Each pending name has exactly one CREATE and one MOVE_FROM on success; its
final has exactly one MOVED_TO.  AUTH has exactly one CREATE after READY
MOVED_TO and before the READY_COMMITTED payload is consumed.  No final name
has DELETE, MOVE_FROM or a second create/move event.  IN_Q_OVERFLOW,
IN_IGNORED and IN_UNMOUNT are fatal.  The retained leaf identity must equal the
event-selected object; same-byte replacement is invalid.

Each event row has exact keys `ordinal, parent_role, name, mask, cookie,
device, inode`, all scalar values encoded as canonical strings.  `mask` is one
of `CREATE`, `MOVED_FROM`, `MOVED_TO`; CREATE has cookie `0`.  A successful
rename has one pending CREATE immediately followed by adjacent pending
MOVED_FROM and final MOVED_TO rows.  The rename rows have the same nonzero
decimal cookie and all three rows have the same retained device/inode.  Event ordinals are
decimal strings forming one contiguous harness-assigned sequence across the
retained parent watches.  `watch_events` is the complete post-install event
inventory for every declared output, transaction and frozen-root child name,
not a transaction-only filter.  The success order is H0 CREATE,
run-main-properties CREATE, READY pending CREATE/rename, AUTH CREATE, PERMIT
pending CREATE/rename, then LEDGER pending CREATE/rename.  Future-output and
`frozen` names have no event before release.  Any other new name or mask in a
retained watched parent rejects; pre-existing directory entries are not
events and remain governed by the frozen directory inventory.

`transaction_state` is an exact seven-object array in table-column order.
Every object has only `role, path, state`; role is one of `authorization,
permit-ready-staging, permit-ready, permit-staging, permit,
permit-ledger-staging, permit-ledger`, and state is `present` or `absent`.

The exact JSON object keys are:

```text
scion.generic_backend.h11_release_authorization.v1
  schema, formal_root, harness_manifest, permit_ready, run_armed, permit_path

scion.generic_backend.h11_permit_ready.v1
  schema, scenario, run_unit, boot_id, invocation_id, harness_manifest,
  run_armed, permit_authority, present_outputs, absent_paths, phase

scion.generic_backend.h11_operator_permit.v1
  schema, scenario, run_unit, boot_id, invocation_id,
  authorization_manifest, harness_manifest, permit_ready, run_armed,
  ready_commit, present_outputs_sha256, future_absence_sha256, phase

scion.generic_backend.h11_permit_ledger.v1
  schema, scenario, run_unit, boot_id, invocation_id, permit_authority,
  authorization_manifest, harness_manifest, permit_ready, permit, run_armed,
  permit_commit, present_outputs, present_outputs_sha256,
  future_absence_inventory, future_absence_sha256,
  pre_ledger_absence_barrier, phase

scion.generic_backend.h11_commit_fifo_receipt.v1
  schema, phase, fifo, payload_sha256, byte_count

scion.generic_backend.h11_absence_barrier.v1
  schema, phase, directory_chain, present_outputs_sha256,
  future_absence_sha256, transaction_state, watch_events

scion.generic_backend.h11_release.v1
  schema, permit_ledger, directory_chain, pre_release_absence_barrier, phase
```

The only phase values are `h11-permit-ready`,
`operator-release-authorized`, `h11-permit-ledger-frozen`,
`ready-committed`, `permit-committed`, `pre-ledger`, `pre-release` and
`h11-release-complete`, each only in its corresponding schema.

All source, present-output, permit and ledger references contain exact
path/SHA-256/device/inode/mode/uid/gid.  FIFO references omit SHA-256 but carry
path/device/inode/mode/uid/gid.  `watch_events` is the canonical ordered event
inventory; `transaction_state` is the exact row above.  Terminal `final` adds
the exact `h11_release` object above.  Its directory chain equals the execution
manifest permit authority, its ledger path is exactly scenario-root/
`PERMIT-LEDGER.json`, and its barrier is the in-memory barrier immediately
preceding release.  Missing or extra keys fail closed.

No failure path releases an acquisition, replaces a transaction file, retries
publication or cleans a partial transaction.  A failed root is poisoned and
requires a new formal root.  No timeout, deadline, sleep, polling, watchdog,
budget, cap or truncation is introduced.

## 5. Required pure acceptance matrix

Before source acceptance, tests must prove:

1. the exact two-present/eleven-absent partition permits one publication;
2. H0 or run-main-properties absent, wrong, replaced or listed as absent is
   rejected;
3. an omitted future role is rejected, including when that omitted output
   exists;
4. a missing, extra or reordered partition item is rejected;
5. a role/path duplicate, transaction-path alias and frozen-root alias are
   rejected;
6. a manifest output-role omission/addition or wrong parent is rejected;
7. a READY partition that differs from the permit authority is rejected;
8. a noncanonical or wrong-layout authorization, staging, READY, permit or
   ledger path is rejected;
9. same-byte manifest/READY/ARMED/present-output/permit/ledger inode
   replacement is rejected;
10. directory-chain symlink, inode or mode replacement, hardlink alias and
    source `(device,inode)` reuse are rejected, including directory
    move-away/move-back signalled by IN_MOVE_SELF;
11. a future output created after validation but before permit publication
   is rejected by the final absence revalidation;
12. a future output created after permit publication, during ledger
    publication or immediately before harness release is rejected by the
    final release-adjacent barrier;
13. READY/permit/ledger staging publication is atomic-visible; injected
    pre-rename failure leaves staging, post-rename/fsync failure may leave
    final, and neither failure emits its commit FIFO payload;
14. threaded pure choreography covers both endpoint scheduling orders without
    sleep/polling; exact READY/PERMIT bytes and identities unblock once, while
    wrong, duplicate or missing bytes do not advance;
15. every transaction phase rejects a wrong present/absent path, unexpected
    event, watch overflow/ignored/unmount or replacement event;
16. duplicate permit publication remains no-replace and no failure releases
    H11;
17. every exact schema rejects missing/extra fields and the permit, harness
    ledger, terminal final and final-freeze receipt bind
    the full ledger reference and both exact canonical digests;
18. rename-success/parent-fsync-failure remains poisoned and cannot produce a
    commit receipt or release;
19. AST closure still proves the absence of automatic release, timeout,
    polling, retry, cleanup, cap and truncation.

Authorizer-negative cases fail before permit publication.  Harness-consumption
negative cases may retain an already published permit and final-barrier
failures may retain a ledger, but all fail before release and poison that
root.  Tests use
temporary-directory function calls or the pure fake manager and exercise the
same named-staging publication primitive as production; they do not grant
privileged-launch authority.

2. add the two commit FIFOs to the root tree/preflight authority;
3. add the exact directory chain, permit authority and canonical derivation to

4. correct the installer authorizer against the same retained manifest bytes;
5. add the complete negative matrix and rerun Python 3.12/3.13 pure suites;
6. integrate the external permit consumption, final barrier, ledger/final and
