## CVRP Frontier

- CVRP now shows useful research-loop behavior: target-intent can pivot away
  from stale directions, branch lessons reach prompts, material solver code is
  generated, formal screening completes, mechanism telemetry is preserved, and
  weak/negative hypotheses can be rejected with evidence.
- CVRP has still not met v0.4 effective-research acceptance because no current
  solver-design branch has produced continuous improvement or promotion.
- Rejected/default-avoid directions: broad VNS removal, pure ALNS/no-polish,
  simple initial-VNS disablement, raw cadence-2, recent-best/stall gating,
  fixed early-8, tested share70 cap/rescue variants, unchanged route-merge
  absorption, unchanged `demand_slack_regret_insertion`, unchanged
  `cross_route_2opt_reconnect`, unchanged `cluster_biased_worst_removal`, and
  unchanged `route_limit_seed_diversification`.
- Construction seed/portfolio effects now require a same-run seed baseline or
  same-mechanism accepted delta; fallback activation is only design evidence.
- Active CVRP solver context now exposes the existing size70 two-opt fallback
  as an active fact and readable scheduler slice; future proposals should treat
  it as a known mechanism unless they change its trigger, budget behavior, or
  polish implementation with direct effect evidence.
- CVRP proposal context now has problem-owned measurement/opportunity
  diagnostics for MDE-vs-practical-delta, low-SNR interpretation, aggregate
  screening headroom, default-avoid mechanisms, and measurable opportunity
  classes. This is proposal-only context and does not feed Decision, Protocol,
  lifecycle, scheduler, or promotion.
- CVRP large-instance two-opt postrun briefs now expose
  `cvrp_large_twoopt_summary`, requiring current-run evidence, review inputs,
  and a large-twoopt mechanism signal before calling the bounded two-opt
  follow-up review-ready.
- Prepared launch `research_focus` now bridges into hypothesis prompt compact
  research signals as proposal-only context. Prepared prompt readiness audits
  both the source-side bridge and real launch environment markers, including
  `launch.env` assignment and generated `run.sh` export of
  `PREPARED_RUN_MANIFEST`. Launch readiness now requires
  `prompt_context_readiness_complete=ok`, including artifact identity for the
  current root/manifest, so this audit is enforced by the operator-facing
  readiness tool before launch.

## Continuation And Reporting Repairs

- Copied campaigns now restore current champion, active branch, workspace,
  active hypothesis, candidate patch, and branch evidence.
- Follow-up case targeting, compact status/progress projection, reduced
  measurement readiness projection, and research-efficiency reports are accepted
  observability repairs.
- Research-efficiency reports now include audit-only research-continuity
  metrics for same-mechanism follow-up selection, branch-lesson
  satisfaction/semantic gaps, weak-positive transfer acceptance, lesson actions,
  and branch shape. Postrun artifact inventories expose that surface as a Phase
  4 evidence-coverage requirement, and postrun analysis briefs summarize the
  current-run continuity metrics for delegated review. Postrun analysis briefs
  also summarize current-run branch/event/hypothesis state,
  protocol/formal-candidate accounting, measurement effect-vs-MDE, prompt
  context/source visibility, code/hypothesis source-visibility guarantees,
  prompt signal density, runtime feedback/drain behavior, and failure
  taxonomy/proposal quality without exposing raw prompts, responses, patches,
  raw logs, source bodies, or mutating runtime semantics.
- These repairs are intentionally report/continuation scoped. They do not change
  Decision, `DecisionFeatures`, Protocol, scheduling, gates, budgets, lifecycle
  policy, proposal context semantics, or problem semantics.
- Warehouse follow-up postrun summaries now require protocol-evaluated evidence
  plus measurement-effect, runtime-feedback, and research-continuity summaries
  before reporting plateau-review readiness; incomplete review inputs stay
  report-only evidence gaps.

## Runtime Semantics Repair

- Budget-exhausting solver runtime semantics are now narrower and less
  misleading for CVRP-style anytime runs. High aggregate
  `runtime_regression_rate` no longer blocks low-SNR trajectory-divergent
  screening expansion, creates lifecycle soft-abandon/repeated-signal noise, or
  assigns a proposal feedback `runtime_regression` tier, vetoes low-signal
  `retain_head` workspace preservation, or creates strong prompt actionability
  when the problem declares `runtime_model=budget_exhausting`.
- This is a deterministic runtime-model interpretation repair, not a generic
  gate tightening, budget change, prompt truncation, or DecisionFeatures change.

## Low-Signal Scheduling Repair

- Low-signal no-effect branches can receive one same-mechanism observation
  sample before a later clean fork, and that selected action is now reported as
  `refine_active` rather than `repair_diagnostic`. Treat true repair diagnostics
  separately from scheduler-selected low-SNR research continuation when reading
  status, lineage, or research-efficiency artifacts.
- Current active no-effect branch-local lessons now become advisory
  `same_branch_refinement` proposal requirements, so same-mechanism follow-up
  must contrast the prior no-effect evidence without converting that lesson
  into Decision input.
- Trajectory-divergent all-tie screening now enters the low-SNR expand/continue
  path when quality and runtime evidence are non-regressive; negative, loss-heavy,
  failed, and runtime-vetoed candidates still fail closed.

## Launcher And Infrastructure State

- CVRP and warehouse launchers now support prepare-only roots,
  completion-preflight, secret-safe API-key env routing, runtime-source guards,
  campaign copy/resume, secret-free prepared-run manifests, prepare-time
  delegated handoff briefs/inventories, and default postrun report generation.
  They also auto-fill deterministic report-only control-pair-key metadata when
  the operator omits it, so prepare-only roots do not fail the prepared
  contract solely because handoff metadata was blank.
  Postrun inventory/brief tooling reports prepared-run contract consistency for
  current local mirrors and WSL launch roots. Prepared-only handoff artifacts
  now mark copied resume snapshots as `prepared_only/not_started` with zero
  current-run effective rounds, so delegated review cannot confuse launch input
  with postrun research evidence. Historical roots can be normalized with the
  report-only postrun acceptance rebuild tool; its manifest records per-family
  success/failure and keeps evidence gaps visible. CVRP prepared handoffs now
  carry the problem-owned MDE/low-SNR/opportunity diagnostics used by proposal
  context. Current launcher postrun paths call the rebuild tool directly,
  including completion-preflight failure exits where copied resume artifacts
  must remain non-current evidence.
  Preflight-failed launch roots now also preserve structured failure
  classification, sanitized auth/account state, login-url presence, operator
  action, and the proxy detail artifact path. Inventory and analysis-brief
  top-level branch/event/hypothesis/LLM-trace fields are current-run scoped;
  copied campaign counts live under `resume_snapshot`.
  `check_launch_readiness.py` is now the operator-facing static/launch readiness
  verifier for prepared roots; completion-preflight failures now include an
  actionable operator step and a proxy login URL when available. Docs-only
  pointer refreshes are accepted only when the manifest's runtime guard paths
  are unchanged.
- The current blocker is LLM infrastructure. The WSL `gpt-5.5` route reaches
  launch-readiness preflight and currently reports `authenticated=false`,
  `active=0`, `total=1`, while the real chat completion returns
  HTTP `401` with `classification=not_authenticated` and
  `code=invalid_api_key`.
  Readiness includes `operator_action.login_url`.
  Do not launch prepared roots until `/v1/chat/completions` succeeds with
  non-empty output after re-login/token refresh.
- Current CVRP and warehouse prepared roots were regenerated from WSL checkout
  `399db52` after prepared analysis brief contract identity became part of
  static launch readiness. The CVRP root carries structured bounded large-instance
  two-opt constraints and postrun briefs now expose
  `cvrp_large_twoopt_summary` so delegated review cannot confuse prepared-only,
  missing-signal, or incomplete-handoff evidence with bounded two-opt review
  readiness. Warehouse follow-up summaries likewise require complete
  problem-specific handoff before plateau-review-ready interpretation, and both
  CVRP/warehouse required review questions explicitly ask about incomplete
  handoff. The prepared briefs now filter those specialist questions by problem
  family, avoiding irrelevant CVRP questions on warehouse roots and irrelevant
  warehouse questions on CVRP roots. Prepare-only briefs now ask reviewers to
  prove launch/readiness/handoff state instead of answering current-run
  research-quality questions, their minimum delegated-analysis sections no
  longer show branch/LLM/Protocol inspection instructions before launch, and
  specialist warehouse/CVRP review axes are deferred until post-launch
  current-run evidence exists. Both
  roots are static-ready, prompt-audit-ready,
  prepared-analysis-brief-ready, problem-specific-handoff-ready, bridge-audited
  against source and launch markers, identity-checked against the current
  root/manifest and prepared analysis brief contract, and prepare-only/not-started.
  Postrun evidence handling now also isolates `invalid_infra_only` roots from
  current-run research evidence, preserving copied or partial artifacts only as
  `resume_snapshot` material and marking warehouse/CVRP review axes
  non-actionable for infra-only roots. Launcher postrun rebuilds also write a
  `POSTRUN_REPORTS_EXIT_STATUS` marker for delegated-review auditability without
  changing campaign validity or solver evidence.
