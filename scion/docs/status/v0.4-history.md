## 2026-06-16 Research-Loop Repair Lessons

- Warehouse longrun regression showed v0.4 was not catastrophically broken:
  runs could reach validation/frozen and promote in some repeats, but did not
  recover v0.3-style continuous improvement. Main bottlenecks were proposal
  quality, branch-lesson usability, same-file edits, and fresh-runtime replay
  pressure.
  Synthesis:
  [`../experiments/v0.4/v04-warehouse-vrp-process-synthesis-20260616.md`](../experiments/v0.4/v04-warehouse-vrp-process-synthesis-20260616.md).
- Hard prompt budgets and truncation were removed from useful research context.
  The accepted direction is to filter noise, not compress away current branch
  lessons or research signals.
- Warehouse pair-level diagnostic validation/lifecycle repairs made the path
  more fail-closed and more auditable, but did not by themselves produce
  accepted research quality.
- Independent VRP controls mostly produced negative or weak external
  hypotheses. They support the conclusion that CVRP/VRP needs mechanism
  diagnostics and better runtime semantics before broad agentic campaigns.

## 2026-06-11 Audit Reset

- The June 11 review set is the main reset point for v0.4 closeout:
  [`../../reports/v04-core-framework-review-20260611.md`](../../reports/v04-core-framework-review-20260611.md),
  [`../../reports/v04-core-framework-code-review-20260611.md`](../../reports/v04-core-framework-code-review-20260611.md),
  and
  [`../../design/v0.5-evidence-uplift-roadmap.md`](../../design/v0.5-evidence-uplift-roadmap.md).
- The main conclusion was not “generic Scion loop is P0 broken.” It was that
  warehouse still had a working path, while CVRP effects were below the
  measurement/gate resolution and needed better problem-owned diagnostics.
- v0.4 therefore must finish framework/readiness repairs and restore effective
  warehouse/CVRP research before v0.5 runs broad controlled experiments.
